Send events to a microVM that wakes and responds in milliseconds, then shuts down until the next event.

<ValueCards>
  <ValueCard title="Cold boots and responds in milliseconds" metric="cold start < 10 ms">
    No warm instance waiting for a delivery that may not come; the event itself is what starts the handler.
  </ValueCard>
  <ValueCard title="Costs nothing between events" metric="zero idle cost">
    Between deliveries the handler isn't running.
    Endpoints that fire a few times a day stop being a standing bill.
  </ValueCard>
  <ValueCard title="Absorb event storms" metric="autoscale in < 10 ms">
    When a provider replays a backlog, the service group [adds instances](/features/autoscale) in milliseconds and [balances](/features/load-balancing) across them.
  </ValueCard>
</ValueCards>

## Under the hood

The platform features this use case is built on.

<FeatureTable>
  <FeatureRow title="Scale-to-zero" href="/features/scale-to-zero">
    The VM only runs while it is actively serving a request.
  </FeatureRow>
  <FeatureRow title="Autoscale" href="/features/autoscale">
    The service group can add instances in milliseconds to deal with event storms.
  </FeatureRow>
  <FeatureRow title="Load balancing" href="/features/load-balancing">
    Distributes incoming events across the instances in the service group.
  </FeatureRow>
</FeatureTable>
