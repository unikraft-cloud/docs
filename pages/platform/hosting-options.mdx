Unikraft Cloud runs in three places.
The availability block at the top of a feature page tells you where that feature works.

| Option | Who runs the nodes | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|
| [Unikraft Cloud](#unikraft-cloud) | Unikraft, in the [metros](/platform/metros) | Everyone with an account |
| [BYOC](#byoc) | Unikraft, in your cloud account | Enterprise customers |
| [On-prem](#on-prem) | You, in your data center | Enterprise customers |

## Unikraft Cloud

Unikraft Cloud is the hosted platform.
Unikraft operates the nodes in the public [metros](/platform/metros), and you deploy with the [CLI](/cli/overview), the [API](/api/platform/v1), or an [SDK](/sdks/overview).
[Sign up](https://console.unikraft.cloud/signup) to get an access token.

Some features need an enterprise plan on Unikraft Cloud.
The availability block shows an **Enterprise** hint on the Unikraft Cloud chip for those features.
To enable one for your account, send an email to [support@unikraft.com](mailto:support@unikraft.com).

## BYOC

With BYOC, short for bring your own cloud, Unikraft Cloud runs on servers in your own cloud account.
You keep the data and the network inside your account, and Unikraft operates the installation.

## On-prem

With on-prem, Unikraft Cloud runs on your own servers in your own data center.
This is the option for compliance programs and for workloads that must not leave your network.

## BYOC and on-prem installations

A BYOC or on-prem installation runs under a license, so every feature on it belongs to an enterprise plan.
Some features exist only on these installations, because they need control of the nodes.
Examples are [live migration](/features/live-migration), [managed network mode](/features/custom-network-configuration#managed-network-mode), and the [local registry](/cli/registries).

Each installation is a metro of its own.
To target it with the CLI, define a [profile](/tutorials/kraftkit-to-unikraft#profile-management) with the API endpoint of the installation, then pass its name with `--metro`.

To discuss a BYOC or on-prem installation, [get in touch](https://unikraft.com/contact).

## Reading the availability block

- **Available on** lists the three hosting options.
  A check mark means the feature works there.
  A clock means it's coming soon.
  A cross means it's not available there.
  A **Differs** hint means the page describes something that behaves differently on that option, and the notes explain how.
- **Interfaces** shows whether the API, the CLI, and the SDKs expose the feature.
- **Notes** give the next step when something is missing.
  A note can name a workaround or a contact address.
